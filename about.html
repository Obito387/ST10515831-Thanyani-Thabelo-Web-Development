Our Story

Built on quality, worn every day.

Founded in 2014 in Pretoria, Urban Threads began as a small family-run rail of curated denim and grew into a full local clothing, footwear, and accessories store.

Every piece is chosen for durability, clean tailoring, and fair everyday pricing—available both in our Troye Street store and via online enquiry.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check product availability?

Choose a product on our enquiry form. Our team confirms stock and sizes before you order.

Where can I visit the store?

Find us at 214 Troye Street, Pretoria. See opening hours and the interactive map.

Can I request a bulk order?

Select “Bulk order” on the enquiry form and include quantities and sizes. Listed prices are indicative; the team confirms your quote.
